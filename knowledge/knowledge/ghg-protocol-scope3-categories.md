# GHG Protocol Corporate Standard + Scope 3 Technical Guidance (Summary)

## Corporate Standard basics
The GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard defines Scope 1 (direct emissions from owned/controlled sources), Scope 2 (indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, cooling — reported both location-based and market-based), and Scope 3 (all other indirect emissions in the value chain). Organizational boundaries are set by equity share, financial control, or operational control — this determines what counts as Scope 1 vs Scope 3 for assets like leased vehicles or franchises.

## The 15 Scope 3 categories (brief)
1. Purchased Goods & Services — emissions from production of purchased goods/services.
2. Capital Goods — emissions from production of purchased capital goods (equipment, buildings).
3. Fuel- and Energy-Related Activities (not in Scope 1 or 2) — upstream emissions of purchased fuels/energy not combusted by the reporting org, plus transmission/distribution losses.
4. Upstream Transportation & Distribution — transportation/distribution of purchased products, paid for by the reporting company.
5. Waste Generated in Operations — disposal/treatment of waste generated at owned/controlled facilities.
6. Business Travel — employee travel for business purposes in vehicles not owned/controlled by the org.
7. Employee Commuting — employee travel between home and work.
8. Upstream Leased Assets — operation of assets leased BY the reporting company (lessee), not already in Scope 1/2.
9. Downstream Transportation & Distribution — transportation/distribution of sold products, paid for by the customer.
10. Processing of Sold Products — processing of intermediate products by downstream companies.
11. Use of Sold Products — end-use emissions of sold products over their lifetime.
12. End-of-Life Treatment of Sold Products — disposal/treatment of sold products at end of life.
13. Downstream Leased Assets — operation of assets owned by the reporting company but leased TO others (lessor).
14. Franchises — operation of franchises not included in Scope 1/2.
15. Investments — operation of investments (equity, debt, project finance) for financial institutions.

Common confusion points: fuel purchased but not combusted by the reporting org belongs in Category 3, not Scope 1; inbound freight paid by the reporting org is Category 4, outbound freight paid by the customer is Category 9; a leased asset is Category 8 if the reporting org is the lessee, Category 13 if the reporting org is the lessor.
