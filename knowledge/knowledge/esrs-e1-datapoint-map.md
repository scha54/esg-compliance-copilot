# ESRS E1 (Climate Change) Datapoint Map — Key Disclosure Requirements

ESRS E1 is the climate-change standard under the EU's Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD). Key disclosure requirements (brief):

- E1-1 Transition Plan for Climate Change Mitigation — whether/how the org has a transition plan compatible with limiting global warming to 1.5°C, including decarbonization levers and key assumptions.
- E1-2 Policies related to Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation — description of policies governing climate mitigation/adaptation.
- E1-3 Actions and Resources in relation to Climate Change Policies — concrete actions taken/planned and resources allocated.
- E1-4 Targets related to Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation — GHG reduction targets, base year, target year, scopes covered, whether science-based.
- E1-5 Energy Consumption and Mix — total energy consumption by source (renewable/non-renewable), energy intensity.
- E1-6 Gross Scopes 1, 2, 3 and Total GHG Emissions — the core emissions inventory, both location-based and market-based Scope 2, all material Scope 3 categories, in tCO2e.
- E1-7 GHG Removals and GHG Mitigation Projects Financed Through Carbon Credits — offsets/removals used, methodology, verification status.
- E1-8 Internal Carbon Pricing — whether/how an internal carbon price is applied.
- E1-9 Anticipated Financial Effects from Material Physical and Transition Risks and Opportunities — forward-looking financial exposure to climate risk.

Materiality: ESRS requires a double-materiality assessment (impact materiality + financial materiality) to determine which datapoints must be disclosed; E1-6 (the emissions inventory) is treated as effectively always material for in-scope entities.
